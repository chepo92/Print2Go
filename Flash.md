Creality box from stock to OpenWRT flash procedure

Download the release files
Open KlipperWrt-x.y.z\Firmware\OpenWrt_snapshot\cxsw_update.tar.bz2
Copy file cxsw_update.tar.bz2 to a fresh formated FAT32 SD card 

Turn off the device, insert sd card, turn on device, it will automatically start flashing the firmware, the LEDs will be turning on and off, after about 5 minutes the flash will be completed

Now proceed to initial setup

Connect to the device with a LAN cable, can be direct connected or through your router
